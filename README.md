# Hey, I'm Atharva 👋

I started learning AI/ML in a very not-so-organized way (random YouTube rabbit holes at 2am, you know how it goes) and somewhere along the way it turned into actually building things.

These days I mostly build AI systems that run locally and hold up in the real world, plus the backends that keep them alive. I like projects where something actually works at the end, not just a cool notebook.

Outside of code, you'll probably find me at a hackathon, losing sleep and surviving on coffee.

## 🛠️ Stuff I've built

**📄 DocuMorph**
Throw PDFs, scanned pages or even phone photos at it and you get clean, structured data back. Runs fully on one 8 GB GPU with zero paid APIs. You can chat with your documents too. Fully open source.

**🛡️ WARRANT**
AI assistants that read your emails and pay your bills can get tricked by instructions hidden inside an email or web page. WARRANT stops that by checking that every sensitive value (payee, email, URL) actually came from you. Blocked 608 of 609 attacks on the AgentDojo benchmark.

**🚆 GatiNishchay** 🏆
Mumbai journey planner that combines local trains, Metro, buses, autos and walking. It checks crowd reports before trusting them, so no rerouting because of some random WhatsApp forward. Also has an AI trip planner and a women's safety SOS.

**🖼️ PixelPure**
Image denoising with a Vision Transformer that runs right in your browser. No server needed.

**🛒 GarageSale**
A marketplace for students to buy and sell stuff, with real-time chat.
